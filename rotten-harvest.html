The Story

The lanterns are lit. The cider is warm.

Amberfield's Harvest Festival is about to crown its winner. Wendell Furrow grew the biggest pumpkin anyone has ever seen. But Wendell is missing. A few travelers passing through get asked to check on him before the festival ends.

They find something terrible at his farm. It didn't stay there. It's already headed for the festival. Amberfield has no idea what's coming. The travelers might be the only ones who can stop it.
