The Story

The lanterns are lit. The cider is warm.

Amberfield's Harvest Festival is about to crown its winner. Wendell Furrow's prize pumpkin is the talk of the town, the biggest anyone has grown in living memory.

But Wendell himself is missing. A few travelers passing through, drawn in by the promise of a warm meal, get asked to check on him before the festival wraps.

What waits for them at his farm is not Wendell, not anymore. Whatever happened to him didn't stay contained to his fields. The festival everyone is celebrating may not survive the morning.
